03

Tooling & CI/CD

I treat the release pipeline as part of the architecture. I've owned this whole chain on more than one team.

A pipeline I own

App Center → Firebase App Distribution → TestFlight

I built and maintain the whole release chain. App Center builds each branch and handles signing and provisioning in one place. Firebase App Distribution sends builds to the right QA tester groups automatically, with release notes generated from commit trailers. TestFlight runs a staged external beta for pilot groups at plants and sites. Crashlytics and Analytics decide promotion: a rollout only moves forward if the crash-free rate and key funnel numbers are at least as good as the previous release.

Signing & provisioning automation Tester group topology Staged rollout Telemetry-gated promotion Crash triage workflow

Observability

Crashlytics with custom keys, and non-fatal reports to catch silent failures. Firebase Analytics funnels for each step of a workflow. Cloud Messaging used to send commands that are safe to repeat, and never to carry data.
